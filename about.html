About Ellipse Hotels

Hospitality should feel effortless — contemporary comfort with genuine human service.

A simple idea, held carefully.

Ellipse Hotels is built around the belief that a good stay is clear, calm and considered. We do not invent a long history here — the hotel’s story will deepen as the property and its people are ready to share it.
